About Perma-Vent

Built for property managers who need documentation, not another vendor relationship.

Perma-Vent Property Protection was founded to solve a specific problem in multi-family property management: unprotected vent stack penetrations that lead to wildlife intrusion, water damage, tenant complaints, and liability exposure.

The company is built around a single service delivered at scale: permanent installation of hurricane-rated pest exclusion covers across entire portfolios, with same-day per-unit warranty documentation.

Every decision in this business — from the product we install to the documentation we deliver — is designed for the property manager who needs to show their board, their tenants, and their attorney that preventive steps were taken and documented.

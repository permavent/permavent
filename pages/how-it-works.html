How It Works

Four steps from first call to fully documented.

The entire process is designed for property managers who need efficiency, documentation, and zero disruption to tenants.

Step 1 — Before the Visit

Portfolio assessment and per-unit quote.

You send us your property details: approximate unit count, number of buildings, property type, and location. We do not need to visit the property for the initial quote.

Within 48 hours, you receive a per-unit quote based on your property profile. The quote includes scope of work, product specifications, and warranty terms. No surprises on installation day.

  • No on-site visit required for the estimate
  • Per-unit pricing delivered within 48 hours
  • Quote includes full scope, product specs, and warranty terms
  • Single point of contact throughout the process

Step 2 — During the Visit

Single mobilization. Every building. One day.

We schedule one visit and install across all buildings in your property. Our crew works building by building, installing Critter Quitter hurricane-rated exclusion covers on every vent stack penetration.

All work is performed on the roof. No tenant appointments. No unit access. No coordination with residents. The installation is invisible to your tenants.

Average per building

Approximately 45 minutes per building for complete installation of all vent stack covers.

Zero. Roof access only. No unit entry. No appointments. No advance notice to residents required.

Step 3 — After the Visit

Same-day documentation delivered.

The day installation is complete, you receive your documentation package. This includes a master property summary and individual per-unit warranty certificates.

Everything is formatted for your property management files. When a tenant, board member, or attorney asks what preventive measures are in place, you produce the documentation. Conversation handled.

What you receive:

  • Master property installation summary
  • Individual per-unit warranty certificates
  • Installation date and scope for each unit
  • Product specifications and coverage terms
  • Transferability documentation

About the product.

Critter Quitter is a hurricane-rated pest exclusion cover designed to seal vent stack penetrations on roofs. It prevents wildlife entry and water intrusion through the openings where vent pipes exit the roofline. The product is made from UV-stable material designed for permanent outdoor installation.
Critter Quitter covers are manufactured from high-impact, UV-stabilized material rated for hurricane conditions. The material does not degrade, crack, or dry out under prolonged sun and weather exposure in Florida conditions.
Critter Quitter products are rated for hurricane-level wind conditions. The installation method secures each cover to the vent stack penetration without modifying the roof surface.
No. Installation does not require any penetration, modification, or alteration of the existing roof surface. The covers are installed over existing vent stack openings. Your roof warranty remains unaffected.
Multi-family units typically have one to three vent stack penetrations per unit, depending on building design. All penetrations on every unit are covered during installation.

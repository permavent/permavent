Warranty Documentation

Your warranty is your proof of action.

Every unit receives an individual warranty certificate. Every property receives a master summary. Everything is transferable and ready for your property file.

What Is Covered

Plain-language warranty coverage.

The Perma-Vent warranty covers two specific risks at every vent stack penetration where a Critter Quitter cover has been installed:

Wildlife entry through covered penetrations

If wildlife enters through a vent stack penetration that has a Perma-Vent installed cover, the warranty applies. This includes squirrels, birds, rodents, and other wildlife that would otherwise access the building through the vent opening.

Water intrusion through covered penetrations

If water intrusion occurs through a vent stack penetration where a Perma-Vent cover has been installed, the warranty applies. This covers rain-driven water entry at the covered penetration point.

What Is Not Covered

Clear exclusions. No fine print.

We state our exclusions clearly so you know exactly what the warranty does and does not cover. There is no ambiguity.

Entry points not covered by the installation

The warranty covers only vent stack penetrations where Critter Quitter covers have been installed. Other roof penetrations, soffits, fascia, or building openings not included in the installation scope are not covered.

Damage from events unrelated to covered penetrations

Roof damage from storms, falling debris, structural failure, or other events unrelated to the vent stack penetrations are not covered. The warranty addresses the specific penetrations sealed during installation.

Modification or removal of installed covers

If installed covers are modified, removed, or tampered with by third parties after installation, the warranty for those specific units is voided. This includes removal during roofing work performed by other contractors.

Pre-existing wildlife already inside the building

The installation closes entry points. It does not address wildlife that may already be inside the building at the time of installation. If pre-existing wildlife presence is suspected, a separate removal service should be arranged before installation.

Transferability

The warranty transfers with the property.

When a property changes ownership or management, the Perma-Vent warranty transfers to the new entity upon written notice. The per-unit certificates and master property summary remain in effect.

Transfer process:

  • Written notice to Perma-Vent of ownership or management change
  • Updated certificates issued to new ownership entity
  • No additional fees for warranty transfer
  • Full original warranty terms remain in effect
Annual Inspection Program

Optional annual validation.

If your records program or property management standards require periodic verification, an optional annual inspection is available. During the inspection, every installed cover is checked and the status of all covered penetrations is documented.

  • Annual inspection of all installed covers
  • Written inspection report for your property file
  • Confirmation of warranty status for each unit
  • Available on request, priced per property
